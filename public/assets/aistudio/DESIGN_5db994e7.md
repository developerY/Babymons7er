## Brand & Style
The design system bridges the mythic grandeur of high-end Hollywood cinematography with the crystalline precision of frontier artificial intelligence research. Built for an autonomous agentic framework operating across seven distinct domains, the aesthetic avoids typical neon-cyberpunk clichés in favor of prestige editorial direction reminiscent of A24, archival fine-press publishing, and bespoke luxury production ateliers.

The core visual philosophy is defined by:
- **Cinematic Poise:** Generous, contemplative negative space framing high-contrast display typography. Every viewport is treated as a 35mm film still—balanced, deliberate, and quiet.
- **Academic Rigor Meets Mythic Lore:** Data-rich agentic logs and complex multi-agent architectures presented with archival clarity, evoking an expeditionary logbook engineered for next-generation intelligence.
- **Luminous Naturalism:** A strict light aesthetic composed of unblemished fine parchment, warm mineral tones, and subtle oceanic patinas. Elements feel physical, timeless, and meticulously curated.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for a daylight, luminous ivory environment. It completely eschews dark surfaces, anchoring instead on warm paper fibers, gilded metallic highlights, and oceanic abyssal accents.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** `#FDFCF7` (Pristine Warm Parchment) serves as the primary canvas, supported by `#FFFFFF` for elevated foreground modules and nested cards. Structural borders and rules rely on `#E8E5DF` (Tuscan Gesso) to preserve breathability without heavy visual friction.
- **Ink & Typography:** High-legibility charcoal `#1A1A1A` acts as the primary text ink, with `#2C3539` (Deep Mineral Slate) for secondary technical notations and metadata. Tertiary captions and passive identifiers sit in `#7A7873`.
- **Prestige Accents:** `#C29B38` (Champagne Gold) and `#8C6239` (Warm Archival Bronze) provide film-title hierarchy, active markers, and elevated micro-details.
- **Guardian Highlights:** Oceanic tonalities distinguish agent operations—`#1E3D4A` (Abyssal Marine) for critical state indicators, tempered by `#4E878C` (Celadon Tide) and `#88B2AC` (Pale Aquamarine) for active telemetry and operational tags.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the intellectual and cinematic tenor. Contrast between the classical, editorial high-contrast serifs and the streamlined, functional geometric grotesk creates immediate pedigree.

- **Editorial Serifs (Headlines & Title Sequences):** Reserved for dramatic narrative beats, guardian identity headers, and marquee figures. Set with deliberate letter spacing and generous line heights to preserve breathing room.
- **Modern Grotesque (Operational UI & Body):** Handles systemic data, model logs, orchestration topologies, and dense narrative text. Clean, open apertures provide clarity even at dense micro-scales.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Transformed to uppercase with wide tracking (`0.12em`), echoing title cards, film credits, and museum cataloging markers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a classical 12-column editorial grid structured with broad cinematic gutters and intentional margins. The viewport is treated as a gallery space rather than an overloaded software dashboard.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays deploy 12 columns with standard `1.5rem` gutters and generous `4rem` outer canvas boundaries. Breakpoints: Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px - 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`), and Widescreen (`> 1440px`).
- **Rhythmic Staggering:** Modules frequently break symmetry; text headers align with narrow 4-column sub-spans while guardian visual assets and agent interaction terminals occupy 8-column broad spans.
- **Negative Space Calibration:** Vertical padding between thematic narrative sections expands up to `space-xl` (`4rem` to `6rem` equivalent), letting complex AI concepts breathe with the composure of cinematic scene transitions.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with luxury editorial standards, depth avoids artificial drop shadows and skeuomorphic bevels. Elevation is expressed via subtle tonal layering, refined edge treatments, and parchment-level illumination.

- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas rests at `#FDFCF7`. Floating modules, guardian dossiers, and terminal views advance forward to pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Modules are enclosed with hairline 1px borders in `#E8E5DF`. Active focus shifts boundaries to `#C29B38` (Champagne Gold) or `#1E3D4A` (Abyssal Marine).
- **Ambient Diffusion:** Where elevation is necessary for overlays, popovers, or floating director controls, depth is communicated through soft, expansive ambient blurs: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02)`.

## Shapes
The shape language takes inspiration from archival slide mounts, fine bookbinding, and gallery frame borders. Clean, tailored contours avoid juvenile bulbous forms.

- **Radius Tier 1 (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, interactive tags, and operational chips.
- **Radius Tier 2 (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to guardian profile cards, content viewports, dynamic execution charts, and modal frameworks.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** Defined strictly by crisp, hairline geometry (1px thickness) to segment multi-agent coordinate maps and tabular telemetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (The Premier Cut):** Solid `#1A1A1A` background with `#FDFCF7` text. 4px corner radius, height of 44px, horizontal padding of 24px. Micro-accent: subtle 1px border ring tinted with `#8C6239` on focus.
- **Secondary (Editorial Rule):** Transparent background with a crisp 1px `#E8E5DF` border, hovering into `#C29B38`. Typography in `#1A1A1A` with wide-tracked letter styling.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain text links accompanied by directional hairline glyphs (`→`), resting in `#2C3539` and transitioning into `#C29B38` with a smooth opacity change.

### Cards & Guardian Dossiers
- **Structure:** Pristine `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by 1px `#E8E5DF`. Generous internal padding (`2rem`).
- **Editorial Film Header:** High-contrast serif title (`headline-sm`) anchored above an ultra-fine divider, coupled with a capitalized domain label (e.g., `DOMAIN // NORTHERN ABYSS`).
- **Telemetry Footers:** Split columns carrying live agent status, parameter weights, and execution coordinates rendered in `code-telemetry`.

### Chips & Agent Status Badges
- **Form:** Pill-shaped or subtle rounded box (`rounded-sm`), 24px height, padding of 4px 10px.
- **Coloration:** Soft celadon/marine washed backgrounds (`rgba(78, 135, 140, 0.08)`) with crisp `#1E3D4A` text. Gold status indicators leverage `#C29B38` with an accompanying 4px glowing dot indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Understated `#FFFFFF` surface with hairline `#E8E5DF` borders. Default height 44px with a 4px corner radius. Focus states illuminate with a 1px `#C29B38` outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square and circular geometries with 1px border lines in `#7A7873`. When activated, fields reveal a solid `#1A1A1A` core or gold accent badge.

### Lists & Telemetry Registers
- **Archival Feed:** Minimalist horizontal rows bordered by bottom hairline rules (`#E8E5DF`). Left-hand index counters rendered in Roman numerals or zero-padded integers (`01`, `02`) in `#C29B38`.
- **Interaction:** Hover transitions shift the row background subtly to `rgba(253, 252, 247, 0.8)` with instant cursor refinement.

### Dedicated Modules: The Seven Seas Console
- **Guardian Matrix Panel:** A balanced 7-column or 7-card responsive array exhibiting the operational state of all SeaDragon agents. Incorporates subtle aquamarine micro-sparklines, task completion vectors, and executive agent overrides styled like a luxury production call sheet.
